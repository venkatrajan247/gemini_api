1. What is cricket?
Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of 11 players. It is governed internationally by the ICC and played on an oval field with a rectangular 22-yard pitch at the center.

2. What are the different formats of cricket matches?
There are three main formats:

Test Matches – Played over five days with unlimited overs; each team bats twice.

One Day Internationals (ODI) – Each team plays a maximum of 50 overs.

Twenty20 Internationals (T20I) – Each team plays 20 overs.

3. What are the basic rules of cricket?

Each team has 11 players.

The game starts with a toss to decide who bats or bowls first.

The batting side tries to score runs; the bowling side tries to dismiss the batters.

An innings ends when 10 wickets fall or the set number of overs is completed.


4. How are runs scored in cricket?

Runs are scored when batsmen run between the wickets.

4 runs are awarded when the ball reaches the boundary along the ground.

6 runs are awarded when the ball crosses the boundary without touching the ground.

Extras include: no-balls, wides, byes, and leg byes.


5. What are the different ways a batsman can be dismissed?

Bowled – Ball hits stumps.

Caught – Fielder catches the ball before it hits the ground.

LBW – Ball hits pad and would have hit the stumps.

Run Out – Batsman fails to reach the crease before stumps are broken.

Stumped – Wicketkeeper removes bails while batsman is out of the crease.

Others – Hit Wicket, Obstructing the Field, Timed Out.


6. What are fielding restrictions in cricket?

Powerplays limit the number of fielders outside the 30-yard circle.

In ODIs: First 10 overs – only 2 fielders allowed outside.

In T20s: First 6 overs – same rule.

Certain rules limit fielders on the leg side to prevent unfair play


7. What is the Decision Review System (DRS)?

Allows teams to challenge on-field umpire decisions.

Uses technology: ball-tracking, UltraEdge/Snickometer, Hot Spot.

Each team has a limited number of unsuccessful reviews per innings.

8. What equipment is used in cricket?

Bat – Made of willow wood.

Ball – Hard leather ball.

Wickets – Three stumps with two bails.

Protective Gear – Helmets, pads, gloves, abdominal guards, etc.

Field – Circular or oval-shaped ground with a 22-yard pitch in the center.


9. Who are the match officials in cricket?

On-field Umpires – Make decisions on the field.

Third Umpire – Reviews decisions using video technology.

Match Referee – Ensures fair play and discipline during the game.


10. What are some key cricket terminologies?

Over – A set of 6 legal deliveries bowled by one bowler.

Duck – A batsman getting out without scoring a run.

Maiden Over – An over in which no runs are conceded.

Century – A batsman scoring 100 runs.

Hat-trick – A bowler taking 3 wickets in 3 consecutive deliveries.
